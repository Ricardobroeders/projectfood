# pf-voice

Project Food speaks as a parent who did the reading: calm, specific, on the parent's side, never
a lecture. Two registers in one product: parent-facing calm, kid-visible playful. The rules live
in the knowledge base, not here. Read them first, every time.

## Step 1: read the brand files

- `knowledge-base-general/wiki/brand/brand-voice.md` (the four dials, registers by surface,
  sentence rules, vocabulary per locale, examples, the three gates)
- `knowledge-base-general/wiki/brand/brand-humour.md` (dry, one line, intros only; where never)
- `knowledge-base-general/wiki/brand/brand-stats-and-claims.md` (the only numbers allowed in
  copy, with sources; the claims we never make; how to cite)

## Step 2: place the piece

Name, in one line, before writing:
- **Surface** (app chrome, notification, celebration, card, store, article, printable, social,
  support) and its limits from the surface table.
- **Register**: parent (default) or kid-visible (celebration sheet, card fact, achievement name).
- **Locales**: en, nl, it (de, fr later). Write each natively; never translate word by word.
  A Dutch line is written by a Dutch parent, not converted from English.

## Step 3: write

- Second person to the adult who holds the phone. The child is never addressed outside the
  kid-visible surfaces.
- Sentence case, digits, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no rhetorical questions in body.
- Answer first; every paragraph opens with its claim.
- Vocabulary from the use / avoid tables: taste, the family, the table, tonight, card,
  achievement (NL prestatie, IT traguardo); never "stamp", "for kids", "fix", "free", "boost".
- Numbers only from the stats page, cited as "(Author, year)". No number there, no number here.
- Humour: at most one dry line, in an intro or a tip. Never in FAQ, meta, notifications,
  referral passages, legal or store copy.

## Step 4: run the three gates

1. **Joy, not guilt.** Would a tired parent at 19:00 feel judged, behind or told off? Cut it.
2. **No health claim for a child.** Nothing promised (gut, immune, growth, weight, mood).
   Behaviour only.
3. **A family app, not a kids app.** Store: nothing "for kids", no child addressed or pictured.
   Site: no named or pictured child.

Then the mechanical check: `!`, `—`, "stamp", Title Case, spelled-out numbers.

## Step 5: deliver

One block per locale, in the order en, nl, it. Where a field has a limit (store fields 30 / 80 /
4,000, notification title 40, card fact 140, card tip 200, meta title 55, meta description 120
to 155), print the character count after the line. State which register and surface you wrote
for. If the piece needed a rule this skill does not have, say so and propose the addition to the
brand file; do not invent one silently.
