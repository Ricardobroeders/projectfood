# App downloads, are our install numbers realistic? (2026-10)

**In one line:** No. The model's first-year installs (NL base 17k, all markets base 53k) are a
top-10% outcome for a new app without ads. A realistic year 1 is about 2k to 8k installs in the
Netherlands (central 4k) and 8k to 30k across four to six markets (central 15k). Apps in this
niche pass 100k only with an audience or a channel that reaches every parent.

Ricardo, 2026-10-12: "are our numbers realistic? the installs over time?" and "100k installs
seems a lot". Two research passes the same day; full notes with every URL in
`raw/market-research-2026-10/` (app-download-benchmarks.md, comparable-app-downloads.md).

## How downloads are spread
- Google Play, all 2.02M listed apps (AppBrain, 2026-10-12, counted): 52% under 1k lifetime
  installs, 75% under 10k, 10.3% over 100k. New-app cohort after about 23 weeks: 35% pass 500,
  13% pass 5,000, 4% pass 50,000; most of it happens in the first four weeks.
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026: 17.3% of new apps reach $1k MRR within two years,
  4.6% reach $10k. Monthly revenue one year after launch: median about $72, top quartile $429,
  top 10% about $2,500. Median MRR growth about 5% a year, top quartile about 80%.
- Divided by $0.5 to $1.2 revenue per install (Adapty puts Health & Fitness at $1.21 in 12
  months), the median subscription app gets about 1k to 2k installs a year, the top quartile 4k
  to 10k, the top 10% 25k to 60k, all markets together (our arithmetic).

## Comparable apps (Play counts its exact installs; App Store ratings per country)
- **Indies with no anchor channel:** 2k to 10k installs in year 1, 10k to 45k after two to three
  years. Plant Points about 35k to 45k in two years (the best "30 plants" indie), Thirty Plants
  20k to 25k in 2.5 years, Pitaya about 12k in three, Playful Eats 10.6k Android in two. Most
  other "30 plants" trackers stay under 1k.
- **One market, one strong channel:** about 10k to 15k a year. Beikost App (DE, creator with
  183k Instagram followers), Svezzy (IT, pediatric nutritionist), Grubsy (DE), Eat 30 Plants
  (AT, a bestselling cookbook author).
- **Dutch scale markers:** Ouders van Nu (DPG Media, new in 2025) 45.8k Android in 19 months;
  GroeiGids (handed out by the consultatiebureau) about 31k Android a year. 17k Dutch installs is
  a third of what a national media brand gets.
- **What took apps past 100k:** an audience built before the app (Solid Starts, 1.6M Instagram
  followers, now 1.8M Android installs); a channel that reaches every parent (GroeiGids,
  ZwangerHap via midwives, Mijn Eetmeter 2.0M and Kies Ik Gezond 578k via the Voedingscentrum);
  media brands; store featuring or awards. A pediatric label alone does not move installs.

## Installs over time
- Not flat. A launch spike in the first month, then a low baseline that grows with ASO and
  ratings (3% to 8% a month is a fair range). Health & Fitness peaks every January and falls
  back by March; summer is the low.
- Suggested year-1 shape for an October launch: Q1 30%, Q2 30% (January), Q3 18%, Q4 22%. Each
  later market restarts the curve from its own launch.
- Product Hunt: about 50 to 1,000 installs, mostly English-speaking and outside NL. It is for
  credibility and backlinks, not Dutch installs.
- Word of mouth: k-factor 0.15 to 0.25 is good, 0.4 great; referral programmes land near 0.05.
  A partner or grandparent joining is not a new household. Expect 0.05 to 0.2, adding 5% to 25%
  on top of the other channels ([[concept-network-effects]]).

## Check it once live
- NL App Store ratings per NL-only app run about 1 per 70 iPhone installs (calibrated on Mijn
  Eetmeter, Kies Ik Gezond, GroeiGids). 17k Dutch installs would show as roughly 80 to 100 NL
  ratings by the end of year 1.
- App Store Connect and Play Console give the real installs per day; replace the scenario
  numbers in the model with them after the first quarter.

## What this changes
- The [[source-research-market-size-2026-10]] goal ladder's stage 2 (15k to 21k Dutch installs)
  is a top-10% year, not a modest one. Market size is not the limit; reach is.
- A salary on subscriptions needs about 2,000 paying users at any time, about 65k installs a
  year at 78% churn: a top-5% outcome. The routes the comparables used are an audience
  (creator co-launch) and an institutional channel (consultatiebureau, Voedingscentrum, schools,
  insurers), which is the B2B2C research already proposed in row 9.
