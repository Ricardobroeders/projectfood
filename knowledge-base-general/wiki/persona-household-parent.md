# The dinner-table parent (household)

**One-liner:** A parent of primary-school-age kids (roughly 3–12) who wants the kids to learn
to eat everything without dinner becoming a battle, holds the phone, pays for the app, and
logs what the household ate while the kid does the tapping. **Primary persona since
2026-09-06** ([[source-family-mode-context]]). Provisional: built from one observation and the
founder's own household; to be validated with five parent conversations.

## Profile
- **Context:** Busy household, two rituals already in place: dinner (daily) and groceries
  (weekly). NL dinner ≈ 18:00, IT ≈ 20:00. Kids may be in the EU school fruit scheme
  (NL Nov–Apr; IT primary 6–11). Member of one or more class WhatsApp groups.
- **Goals:** Kids who try new things; fewer arguments at the table; a sense that the family
  eats well without a diet or a lecture; something the kids *want* to do.
- **Frustrations:** "Mijn kind lust geen groente." Clean-plate stand-offs; cooking twice; guilt
  from parenting content; apps that are either therapy tools or reward shops (points for
  screen time); anything that needs a child to have a device or an account.
- **Behaviors:** Asks "what did you eat today?" at pick-up or dinner already; shares things that
  work in the class group; shops on a weekly rhythm; will not "add to home screen", will install
  from an App Store link a friend sent; searches in the moment of frustration
  ("kind eet geen groente tips").

## What they need from Project Food
- A one-minute dinner ritual on the parent's phone where the kid taps and sees the reward
  (card unlock, family progress) — _(evidence: birthday observation, [[source-family-mode-context]] §3.1)_.
- A cooperative family goal, never sibling-vs-sibling — _(D4, pillar 4)_.
- Something forwardable: a class challenge link, a milestone card, printable cards —
  _(D6, D8; [[concept-word-of-mouth]])_.
- A Sunday nudge that makes the shopping decision for them: what the family has not had in
  three weeks and how to serve it so kids will taste it — _(D11; [[concept-stickiness-moat]])_.
- A measurable outcome over months, not a weekly number: the kid's taste count per plant and
  "plants eaten now vs at start" — _(PWA data: reaching 30 did not retain,
  [[source-supabase-metrics-2026-09]]; mechanic in [[concept-retention-loop]])_.
- Plain privacy: one parent account, no child profiles online — _(§3.3)_.
- No health claims, no pressure, no "fix your picky eater" — _([[concept-brand-pillars]])_.
