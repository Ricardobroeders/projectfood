# SEO — live audit and growth plan (2026-09-29)

**In one line:** the article engine works and the learn hub is technically clean; the home page,
the legacy articles and the metadata still sell the adult tracker with a health claim, the site has
no images, no authority and no measured baseline, and the nightly routine runs out of queue on
about 2026-10-01. The plan is one week of hygiene, one decision on the home page, and a queue of
about sixty rows that keeps the engine writing until the end of the year.

## 3. Findings: technical

### 3.1 Fixed today: home URLs advertised with a trailing slash
`lib/marketing.ts` and `app/sitemap.ts` declared `/en/`, `/nl/`, `/it/` as canonical, hreflang and
sitemap URLs while the server 308-redirects them to the slash-less form. Search Console reported
"Page with redirect, discovered through sitemaps" for all three home pages, and hreflang pointing
at a redirect is dropped, so the home pages had no working language cluster. Fixed and verified
live in commit `caf3928`. The remaining "page with redirect" rows (www, http, trailing slash) are
correct behaviour and stay in the report permanently.

### 3.2 Soft-404 wildcard: any dotted path renders the English home page
`https://projectfood.dev/foo.txt` returns **200** with the English home page and `<title>Project
Food — Good gut, good life.</title>`. Cause: the middleware matcher skips every path with a file
extension, so the request reaches `app/[locale]/page.tsx` with `locale = "foo.txt"`;
`generateStaticParams` lists en/nl/it but `dynamicParams` is not set to `false`, and the messages
loader falls back to English. Any scanner URL (`wp-login.php`, `sitemap.txt`, `.env`) becomes an
indexable duplicate of the home page. `/nl/foo` correctly returns 404 and `/foo` goes to `/login`
(noindex), so the hole is only the dotted root path. Fix: `export const dynamicParams = false` in
`app/[locale]/layout.tsx`, or a locale guard that calls `notFound()`. Effort: minutes.

### 3.3 The locale cookie disables the CDN
`middleware.ts` sets `pf_locale` on every marketing response. A `Set-Cookie` makes the response
uncacheable, so every page shows `cache-control: private, no-cache, no-store` and
`x-vercel-cache: MISS` on every request, although all marketing routes build as static (`●` in
the build output) and the learn pages declare `revalidate = 3600`. Measured impact today is small
(root document 20 to 30 ms in Lighthouse; the crawl saw 340 to 2,010 ms end to end including
TLS from the Netherlands), so this is a cost and cold-start issue, not a ranking one. Fix: set the
cookie only where the language is chosen (the switcher, the root redirect) and never on a plain
render. Effort: an hour, plus a check that the PWA still picks the language up.

### 3.4 Placeholder pages live
`/en/recipes`, `/nl/recepten`, `/it/ricette` return 200 with "This page is being built" (56 words),
are out of the sitemap and the menu since 2026-09-26 but still routable and indexable. The
non-goal decision stands ([[seo-content-types]]); remove the route and let them 404, or 410.

### 3.6 Structured data
Every article emits `Article` (with `mainEntityOfPage`, `inLanguage`, `wordCount`, `datePublished`,
`dateModified`, `isPartOf` / `hasPart`), `Person`, `Organization`, `BreadcrumbList` and
`FAQPage`. Gaps:
- `Article.image` is absent on every page. Google lists it as required for the Article rich
  result; more practically, an article without an image has no Discover eligibility and no
  image-search presence. The plant renders are the obvious source (§6, batch 0).
- `Organization.logo` is `/icons/logo.svg` on the home page and `/images/logo.png` on the
  articles: one entity, two logos. Unify on `organizationNode()` from `lib/seo.ts`.
- No `sameAs` on `Organization` or `Person` (`AUTHOR_SAME_AS` is empty; Ricardo supplies the
  links). No `SoftwareApplication` node yet (waits for a public store URL).

### 3.7 Performance and accessibility (Lighthouse 12, mobile)

| Page | Perf | A11y | BP | SEO | LCP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/nl` | 91 | 88 | 96 | 92 | 3.4 s | LCP element is the fruit strip PNG (145 KB mobile, 200 KB desktop, `unoptimized`); 365 KiB saved with next-gen formats, 340 KiB with sizing |
| `/nl/leer/alles-leren-eten/hoe-vaak-proeven` | 100 | 90 | 100 | 92 | 1.5 s | LCP is the H1; nothing to do |

Shared findings: the viewport carries `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no` (`app/layout.tsx`),
which blocks pinch zoom and fails the audit on every page; `#F5C518` text on the cream hero
background measures 1.42:1 and the `#A39B91` meta text (byline, breadcrumb, footer) 2.39 to
2.74:1 against 4.5:1 required; the footer logo's alt duplicates the adjacent "Project Food"
text. Lighthouse also flags `meta-description` on both pages although the tag is present in the
HTML; treat that one as a false positive until the URL inspection tool says otherwise. Fonts
are self-hosted through `next/font`; first-load JavaScript is 139 kB; HSTS is on.

### 3.8 Things that are right and should stay
Localised slugs with 308 from the internal names; reciprocal hreflang with `x-default` on all 44
URLs (0 problems in the crawl); a sitemap with real `lastmod` on the 23 database pages and none on
the static ones; `robots.txt` that disallows the app routes and links the sitemap; `/login` and
the 404 page carry `noindex`; one H1 per page; visible FAQ accordions mirrored in `FAQPage`;
`Person` author linked to the About page; ISR with on-demand revalidation from the publish
script; British spelling with the American name in brackets on the EN pages.

### 4.2 Internal linking
The family hub links well: pillar → every cluster in the body and in the card list, cluster →
pillar in the first paragraph and two related cards, hub → pillars. The weak spots are the
legacy pages (1 to 2 inbound links, only from the hub and their own pillar) and the fact that no
marketing page links into any article: the home page has 116 words and no path to the learn hub
beyond the menu item. Once the home page is rewritten (batch 1), one paragraph with three links
to the pillar and the two strongest clusters per locale is the cheapest authority transfer on the
site.

### 4.3 Brand and entity
The brand query was lost on 2026-09-22 to a catering-equipment firm, a UK charity, a food-bank
Facebook page and a YouTube channel ([[seo-serp-landscape]]). A US web search for
"projectfood.dev" on 2026-09-29 returns the GitHub repository `Ricardobroeders/projectfood` and
`projectfood.it`, not the site. Nothing on the site says "app", "family" or "kids" in a title or
H1; `Organization` and `Person` have no `sameAs`; there is no public store listing to point at
yet (Play closed test since 2026-09-27, iOS in review). The About page is well written but
still ends in "30 plants a week, backed by science, made into a game", which is the adult
pitch and a claim the writing kit rules out.

### 4.4 Backlinks
Unmeasured. No tool was available; the only known referring domain is Ricardo's own GitHub
repository. The plan treats the site as having zero authority, which is the safe assumption for
a domain whose content is 12 days old.

3. **Nothing outside pillar 1 is measured**: not pillar 2 ("30 plants", "what counts"), not

## 6. The plan: one week of hygiene, one decision, then the queue

### Principles
- **The routine is the machine.** Fifteen pages in twelve days came from one skill and one
  cron. Scaling means keeping its queue full and its rules tight, not writing by hand.
- **Depth before breadth.** Every locale keeps the pillar-and-clusters shape; new pillars open
  only when the queue for the current one is written.
- **No health claims, no children's data, no recipes, no seasonal calendars.** Unchanged from
  [[seo-overview]]. The plant pages carry a kid fact and a serving tip, never nutrition.
- **Images are the missing half.** The 224 clay renders are the one asset no competitor has;
  from batch 0 onwards every article and every share carries one.
- **Fits the one thing.** None of this touches the app or the tester clock
  ([[decision-2026-09-29-one-thing-way-of-working]]); it is the fourteen-day window used well.

### What to expect
A domain with no links and twelve-day-old content ranks on specificity first. Realistic:
- Brand query won within weeks of a public store listing plus batch 1.
- NL and IT KD ≤ 15 terms on page 1 within two to four months of publishing, page 2 sooner.
- EN list and chart terms (KD 15 to 20) three to six months, and only with the printables
  actually linked from outside.
- Indexed pages: 44 today, about 80 after batch 2.1 to 2.6, about 200 after the plant pages.
The KPI table in [[seo-roadmap]] carries these as the Dec 2026 and Apr 2027 columns.

### What we will not do
Recipes, seasonal calendars, medical picky-eating content, anything that names or pictures a
child, adult gut-science expansion, paid links, AI-generated pages without the check script and
the brand gates. Unchanged from [[seo-content-types]].
