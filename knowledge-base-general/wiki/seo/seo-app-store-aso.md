# SEO — App Store & Google Play (ASO) for the native launch

**In one line:** With native iOS/Android decided (D1), the App Store listing becomes the
primary conversion surface: it is what gets pasted into a class WhatsApp group. Get the
listing right in NL, IT and EN before anyone links to it; store search is a bonus, not the
plan.

## What the listing has to do
1. Convert a parent who arrives from a shared link in under 20 seconds (screenshots do this).
2. Read as a **family** app on the parent's phone, never a kids' app (no Kids Category, rated
   4+; file §3.3).
3. Be findable for the few store searches that matter per language.

Notes: Apple indexes name + subtitle + keyword field per locale and ignores duplicates; the
NL and IT locales must be real local keyword research, not translations. On Google Play the
long description is indexed, so the NL/IT descriptions carry the parent-problem phrasing
("kind lust geen groente") naturally, once.

Short description drafts for Play (80 characters, the subtitle line first): EN "Kids taste, you
tap. A card for every plant your family tries, together." · NL "Kinderen proeven, jij tikt. Een
kaart voor elke plant die je gezin proeft." · IT "I bimbi assaggiano, tu tocchi. Una carta per ogni
pianta che la famiglia prova."

**Template (one Figma component, five instances).** Frame 1080 × 1920. Background: one of the
icon's four pastels per frame, alternating so neighbouring frames differ. Headline Plus Jakarta
Sans ExtraBold 68 px, ink `#1F1B16`, centred, 120 px from the top, max two lines (the carousel
thumbnail is about half a phone wide, so anything under 60 px stops being readable). Sub-line
Medium 34 px, ink2 `#6B645C`. Phone from y ≈ 420, 820 px wide (screen 780 = 390 pt at 2×),
bleeding off the bottom edge, 20 px ink bezel, no shadow (design taste). Android status bar at
18:32. The UI inside is in the listing's language with the mock family below. The accent must be
the shipped one and row 2 (blue vs teal) is still open, so bind the frames to the theme variable
and re-export after that decision.

**Mock family and numbers** (the same across all frames of a set): parent + two kids. EN Anna /
Mia (Bunny avatar) / Leo (Robot); NL Sanne / Noor / Finn; IT Giulia / Sofia / Luca. Week 17 of
30, per member 14 / 11 / 9, streak 6 dinners. First kid: 23 foods tried, 6 silver, 2 gold, 11 of
72 levels. Strawberry tastes: kid 1 six (silver), kid 2 two (bronze), parent eleven (gold). The
kid fact and tip are the real `plant_facts` rows (status generated; row 13 review may change them).

**Rules.** No child faces or photos: clay renders and UI only. No empty states. No "free" claims
(the paid plan comes later and a stale frame is worse than none). Each locale gets its own set
with the app in that language: en-GB is the default listing, nl-NL and it-IT the launch markets.

**Play Console specs (checked 2026-09-20).** Phone screenshots: 2 to 8, JPEG or 24-bit PNG
without alpha, 1080 × 1920 (9:16). The long side may not exceed twice the short side, so
1080 × 2400 is rejected. At least four at 1080 px or more to be eligible for featuring. Feature
graphic 1024 × 500 is required: wordmark plus the tagline ("Kids taste, family collects" /
"Kinderen proeven, gezin telt" / "I bimbi assaggiano, insieme") on a pastel with three or four
clay renders, not the icon again. Icon 512 × 512 32-bit PNG (`assets/brand/store/play-icon-512.png`
exists). Text fields: app name (30), short description (80), full description (4000); Play has
no subtitle, so the subtitle line opens the short description. Timing: the listing has to be
complete before a closed-testing release, so the frames come after the accent decision and before
backlog item 11.

## Ratings & reviews
- Ask for a rating only after a **card unlock** or a **family 30**, never at onboarding and
  never after a notification (mirrors D9 for push permission).
- Reply to every review in the reviewer's language.

## Web ↔ store plumbing (see [[seo-technical-audit]])
- Smart App Banner (`apple-itunes-app` meta) and Google Play install prompts on the marketing
  site once the apps exist.
- **Universal Links / App Links** for class invites: a `/klas/<code>` link must open the app if
  installed, else the store, else the web fallback. This is the class channel's plumbing.
- `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD with both store URLs; App Store badges in the hero.
- Deep-link the share cards (milestone, card unlock) to the same handler.

## Measurement
- App Store Connect / Play Console: impressions → product page views → installs, by source
  (web referrer vs store search vs direct link). The **direct-link conversion rate** is the
  class-channel KPI.
- Per-class UTM/referral code on the invite link so replication in a second class is
  measurable (file §6 sequence).
